# Readiness P3 — what the kernel lacks (gap list, 2026-09-25)

**Status (2026-09-25):** the owner chose the sheet's P3. G1–G4 and G6's
workaround are built (p1_expected section 25): part 1 proves as
`kernel/problems/taylor/P3_LOWER.json` and `P3_UPPER.json`, with G5 met by
taylor_lagrange's `at` argument (p = b²). G7 and G8 remain, for stage 2.

The sheet's P3 (`readiness/problems.html#p3`, "Fourth order, with the
remainder pinned down"): with γ(β) = (1 − β²)^(−1/2) on 0 ≤ β < 1,

1. γ(β) = 1 + ½β² + ⅜β⁴ + R(β), and by the Lagrange remainder
   (5/16)β⁶ ≤ R(β) ≤ (5/16)(1 − β²)^(−7/2)β⁶;
2. at β = 0.1, R ∈ [3.125, 3.237]·10⁻⁷, checked against γ(0.1) directly;
3. ln cos x = −x²/2 − x⁴/12 + O(x⁶), checked at x = 0.1.

DESIGN.md's own numbering disagrees: line 454 calls the orbit integral
∫ dθ/(1 + e cos θ) "readiness P3"; lines 2819 and 4924 call it P2, as the
sheet now does. §17's gate names "P1, P3, P5". The stage-1 kernel list
(§17) has no series rules; `taylor_lagrange` and `big_O` (§6.7) sit in
stage 2's "series and limit rules". So the sheet's P3 is not provable by
stage 1's kernel, by the design's own plan. Which P3 the gate means is the
owner's decision; this file records what the sheet's P3 would need.

## What already works (probed)

- `deriv` differentiates 1/sqrt(1 − u) and ln(cos u) in u, emitting
  1 − u > 0 and cos u > 0; iterating it gives f', f'', f''' (hint rung 3's
  derivatives), each checkable against the course's form by `field`.
- Goals may be order judgements (`<=`, `<`, GRAMMAR §6), and discharge
  decides order obligations (`farkas`, `sign`, `sign product`,
  `sign node`).

## Gaps

| # | Gap | Needed by | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | **No move proves an order goal.** Every move ends in `ftc` or `close`, which close `==`. A goal `lo <= t @ D` has no move. | 1, 2 | kernel: a move |
| G2 | **`taylor_lagrange` does not exist.** The rule, stated without an existential ξ as §6.7 asks ("remainder with an explicit ξ-bound"): for f ∈ C^(n+1)([0, u]) and m ≤ D^(n+1) f(s) ≤ M for s ∈ [0, u], m·u^(n+1)/(n+1)! ≤ f(u) − Σ_(k≤n) f^(k)(0)u^k/k! ≤ M·u^(n+1)/(n+1)!. The kernel computes the Taylor polynomial by iterated `deriv` and substitution at 0; the bounds m, M are obligations. | 1 | kernel: a rule |
| G3 | **Regularity stops at C¹.** §6.9's built subset is C⁰/C¹; G2 needs C³ of 1/sqrt(1 − u) on [0, u]. | 1 | kernel: regularity |
| G4 | **No monotonicity.** The bounds (1 − s)^(−7/2) ∈ [1, (1 − u)^(−7/2)] for s ∈ [0, u] come from the function increasing; discharge decides signs of expressions, not comparisons between a function's values at two points. Either a `monotone` rule (D f ≥ 0 on [a, b] ⊢ f(s) ≤ f(b)) or a sign certificate for (1 − s)^(−7/2) − 1 ≥ 0 and (1 − u)^(−7/2) − (1 − s)^(−7/2) ≥ 0 with s ≤ u. | 1 | kernel: a rule or a discharge method |
| G5 | **Composition u = β².** The course proves the bound in u and reads it at u = β². A `fact` comes only from the entries table (`fact` mints a handle per lineage, `kernel.py` lines 19–24), and a closed state's `theorem` cannot be bound in another proof, so a bound proved in u cannot be reused at β². Alternatively take Taylor in β to order 5, which needs D⁶γ bounded, a heavier G4. | 1 | kernel: lemma binding, or none |
| G6 | **Rational exponents.** `(1 - u)^(-1/2)` is refused `deriv-no-rule`; the sheet's form must be written 1/sqrt(1 − u), and (1 − u)^(−7/2) as 1/((1 − u)^3·sqrt(1 − u)). A notation gap, not a soundness one. | 1 | parser or authoring |
| G7 | **Decimal evaluation of γ(0.1).** 1/sqrt(0.99) against an interval is `enclose()` and certified numbers (§17 stage 2). The rational bounds 5/16·10⁻⁶ and the upper bound 0.99^(−7/2)·… are exact but irrational in the second case. | 2 | stage 2 |
| G8 | **`big_O` with explicit constants and series composition.** ln cos x = −x²/2 − x⁴/12 + O(x⁶) needs `big_O` (§6.7), composition of two expansions, and a bound for the O term. Or, restated like part 1: ln cos x as Taylor to order 5 with a bounded sixth derivative on [0, x], which is G2 again with a messier G4. | 3 | kernel: stage 2 |
